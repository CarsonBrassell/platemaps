The Reaction Engine

Every plate you've posted, badged where something happened, against a rank that visibly moves. This one runs — and it's now a grid of choices: five layouts × five arrival choreographies, every combination live. Pick a shape, pick a tempo, then fire upvotes, land comments, and push a plate across its milestone.

The motion follows five rules pulled from the apps that do this best — and one from a failure.

Rule 1 · Duolingo Rare is what makes it powerful Duolingo gates its big animations to milestone days only — redesigning one such moment moved day-7 retention +1.7%. So here, an ordinary upvote gets a small pop; the full-screen ceremony fires only when a plate crosses 25 / 100 / 500. duolingo.deconstructoroffun.com
Rule 2 · Neuroscience Anticipation beats receipt Dopamine peaks before the reward, on the prediction gap. The count-up holds a 350ms beat on your old total before it rolls — the pause is the point, not a loading delay. neurolaunch.com · diygenius.com
Rule 3 · Game feel Squash, stretch, never linear Badges arrive with overshoot-and-settle (scale 0 → 114% wide → settle), floats ease out, meters fill on a decelerating curve. Linear motion reads as dead; every curve here has a body. gamedeveloper.com · valdemird.com
Rule 4 · Apple's mistake One celebration at a time Fitness users report their favorite ring animation almost never plays — it collides with simultaneous badges and gets skipped. Everything here runs through a queue: events landing together play in sequence, never on top of each other. discussions.apple.com
Rule 5 · Peak–end End on the best thing The load sequence is ordered so the last beat is the rank track filling — what all of it means — not a trailing badge. The sequence has an ending, not a fade-out. appcues.com
Rule 6 · House rule Honest numbers only Everything animated here is a real event the database recorded — awardPoints' own awarded flag gates every +N, so a re-vote Postgres refused never floats a phantom point. No fake pings, ever. The dopamine loop research also documents the dark side; this system takes the craft and leaves the manipulation. unpluggedpsych.com · PRODUCT.md

Why each beat is the way it is
The badge sits on the plate, not in a list. Which plate people are reacting to is the actual question; a chronological feed never answers it at a glance.
Two tempos only. Acknowledgement is 160ms, ceremony is 620ms+. When everything animates at a middle speed, nothing feels important.
The meter is the hook that's honest. Rank rungs are hundreds of points apart, but some plate is always 4 upvotes from a milestone — near-term pull without inventing anything.
Tapping a badged tile clears its badge. Resolution is part of the loop — a signal you can't dismiss is nagging, not rewarding. Try it.
Reduced motion strips every animation and prints final states. The page must be fully legible with zero motion, so motion is never load-bearing.
On the real phone body, the milestone ceremony gets one short haptic; nothing else does. Same rarity rule, different sense.
