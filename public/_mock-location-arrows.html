My location — the arrow family

Six more, all pointing the same way (38°, north-east) so the shapes can be compared rather than the angles. Same block as the first five.

F

Navigation puck

The dot with a swept arrowhead riding just off its edge — the shape every phone already reads as "facing". Arrow and dot are separate marks, so the dot alone is the honest fallback when no heading is available.

Needs compass
G

The app's own arrow

Literally VOTE_ARROW_PATH from icons.tsx, outline, rotated to the heading. Reads as PlateMaps instantly — but that mark currently means "vote", and DESIGN.md is explicit that it is one gesture with one mark. Borrowing it here spends that.

Needs compass · costs the vote mark
H

Cone with a tip

Treatment E plus a small chevron at the cone's outer edge. The cone says roughly where you are pointed, the tip says exactly — which matters when the compass is noisy and the cone alone looks like it is swimming.

Needs compass
I

Neon arrow

The same tube-lit voice as the restaurant signs, bent into an arrow instead of a word. No fill anywhere — it is drawn light, like everything else on these tiles, and it is the only one of the six that would look at home next to a neon name.

Needs compass
J

Compass needle

A two-tone needle through the dot: lit half forward, dark half behind. Smallest footprint of the six and the least likely to cover a restaurant, and it says "instrument" rather than "you are a spaceship".

Needs compass
K

Course trail

The arrow points along the way you are moving, with a short breadcrumb tail behind it. The direction comes from the GPS fix itself, so this is the one arrow on the page that needs no compass and no second permission — but it only exists while you are walking.

No extra permission · only while moving
