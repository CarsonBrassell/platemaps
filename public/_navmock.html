What our nav could look like

Ten directions for the top-level menu, each lifted from a real pattern on a site I opened, then redrawn in our own Warm Editorial clothes — tan oval track, orange selected pill, orange circle for compose. Nothing here is built yet.

Where we are today

Five slots in one oval, mirrored by the phone's bottom bar. Profile appears twice — as a pill and as the avatar — and the city is dead text.

The ten

Each card names the site the pattern came from, what it buys us, and what it costs.

01

Map takes the fifth slot

after Instagram · Beli — five slots, compose centred

FeedDiscover + MapProfile

Smallest possible move. Map-first social discovery is one of our three stated differentiators, yet the map is currently buried as a tab inside Feed. This promotes it to a peer.

Cost: Friends drops out of the top level and has to live behind the avatar — and it's the one slot carrying a notification dot.

02

Nouns, not screens

after Letterboxd — films · lists · members · journal

PlatesSpots + PeopleLists

Letterboxd names things, not places in the app. Ours would say plainly what the unit of truth is: a plate. It also gives the mono voice something worth setting.

Cost: "Lists" is a capability we don't have yet — it would need saved plates to become a real destination first.

03

City-first

strongest fit

after The Infatuation and Resy — both promote a live city selector into the top bar and hide the rest

San Diego ▾ DiscoverFeed + Friends CL

Both premium food sites I opened lead with the city and demote everything else. On Infatuation the entire top-level IA is the city chooser; Resy puts "Denver" beside the logo before any content link. We already print "San Diego, CA" — this makes it a control instead of decoration, and quietly answers the open question of whether San Diego is the first market or the only one.

Cost: a city selector with one city in it is a promise. Either it ships greyed with "more soon", or it waits until there's a second market. Also drops the Profile pill — the avatar already goes there.

04

Explore-led

after AllTrails — explore → cities · trails · points of interest

ExploreMap + FeedSaved

AllTrails is the closest structural cousin we have: a map product where browsing and saving matter more than social. Saved is a real action in our app with nowhere to land.

Cost: "Explore" is vaguer than "Discover", and this pushes the social half of the product down a level.

05

Answer the question literally

our own product purpose, in nav form

TonightNearby + FeedFriends

"Tonight" = open now, sorted hot. "Nearby" = the map by proximity. The nav stops describing the software and starts answering "what should I eat tonight?" — which is the whole product.

Cost: "Tonight" is wrong at 11am. Would need to shift with the clock, and we already compute open state in Pacific — so it's possible, but it's real work.

06

Editorial verticals

after Resy — the hit list · wine · guides

Our PicksNear Me + Feed

Resy's top level is curation, not features. We already have an "Our Picks" strip on Discover — this promotes it from a shelf to a destination and leans into the editorial half of the aesthetic.

Cost: curation needs a curator. Right now "Our Picks" is a short seeded list, and a top-level tab sets an expectation of regular editorial output.

07

Four slots, avatar owns the account

the smallest honest fix

FeedDiscover + Friends CL

Today Profile is reachable twice from one row — a pill and the avatar next to it. Nearly every site I looked at lets the avatar carry the account alone. Removes a duplicate without inventing anything.

Cost: the phone bar has five slots and looks best balanced around the centre circle; going to four up top means the two bodies stop matching exactly.

08

Two ranks, visibly different

after Letterboxd and Eater — a thin global row over a content row

DiscoverFeed + Friends HotOpen now Top ratedMap

Keeps our rule that the three ranks of control never wear the same clothes: pills for where you are, underlined text tabs for what you're filtering. Makes the map reachable without spending a global slot on it.

Cost: two stacked rows is a lot of chrome above the fold on a phone, and the fold is where the decision gets made.

09

Compose as a labelled pill

after Substack / Strava — name the action rather than drawing a plus

FeedDiscoverFriends Post a plate

Pulls the primary action out of the navigation entirely, so the oval is purely "where you are" and the orange is purely "the thing to do". A bare + never says what you'd be posting.

Cost: loses the centred circle, which is the single most distinctive thing about the current header — and the thumb-position argument on mobile is genuinely strong.

10

Leaderboard promoted

I'd not ship this

after Untappd / Strava — gamification at the top level

FeedDiscover + RanksProfile

Included so it's visibly considered. Points and the leaderboard are the reason fresh local plates keep arriving, and Untappd puts exactly this at eye level.

Cost: it contradicts our own principle — points are a supply mechanism, not the reason the product wins, and a top-level slot is the loudest possible claim that they are. It also outranks the decision the visitor actually came to make.
