PLATEMAPS · FOOD FEED · 09 DIRECTIONS

Feed redesign concepts

Nine directions for the feed, all inside the existing system — cream ground, white cards, one orange accent, Fraunces for names, mono for machine values. They range from a re-hierarchy of the current card to full layout changes. Mockups use tone blocks where photos would sit, exactly as the app does.

CONCEPT 01 · LOW EFFORT

The Plate Ledger

The subject becomes the headline: a dish with its percent, or a restaurant with its stars. Today the card opens with the poster; here it opens with a Fraunces dish title and its verdict, newspaper-style, with the poster demoted to a mono byline. The feed reads as a ledger of plates rather than a stream of people.

KEEPS Single column, current card anatomy, all actions.
CHANGES Order of the header — title first, author second.

CONCEPT 07 · HIGH EFFORT · RULE-BENDER

The Poster

CONCEPT 09 · MEDIUM EFFORT · DESKTOP-LED

The Rail

Discover / Friends / Map move from tabs into a left rail from xl up — and the rail is what finally gives Saved a visible home (today it's a hidden ?view=saved mode) and re-sites the pending-friend-request badge that vanished when the old SideNav was archived. Selecting Map lets the content area break out of the 640px column, which the night map has always deserved. Styled as vertical rank-2 tabs — ink text, short orange bar — deliberately not dressed like the global nav. Below xl the rail folds back into today's tabs: the same one-switch-two-bodies pattern as the header/MobileNav split, and the same maintenance cost.

MY READ

Where I'd start

Updated for two product facts: a post is either a dish review (0–100%) or a restaurant review (1–5 stars), and Discover is going photo-scarce — photos become a friends-only, opt-in surface. That flips the field. The Discover card has to be built from names, numbers and words, which is exactly what 01 (Plate Ledger) — now shown with a photo-less star-review variant — and 05 (Kitchen Ticket) are; 02 and 04's dense digest rows survive photo-scarcity just as well. 03 (Gallery) and 07 (Poster) are photo-dependent, so they stop being Discover options and become Friends-tab treatments — arguably better ones, since that's where the photos will live. My pick: 01 or 05 for Discover, 06 (with photos) for Friends, and 09's rail to hold it together on desktop.
