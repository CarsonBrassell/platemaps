PLATEMAPS · NIGHT MAP · NAME INDICATORS

The nametag, five ways

Five different answers to "what is this dot called?" — all on the same real corner of Fifth & Island, all honoring the night world. Hover any card to replay its entrance. Whichever wins becomes both the hover tag on bare dots and the always-on tag riding every comment bubble.

01 · THE PILL
The bubble's little sibling — same warm card, same hairline. Everything that names something is light.
02 · THE GHOST
No box at all — just type glowing faintly over the street, like the map's own labels waking up. Quietest of the five.
03 · THE MARQUEE
The name in the display voice — Fraunces over a short orange bar, like the constellation names. Proper names get the serif everywhere else; why not here.
04 · THE CALLOUT
An architectural leader line that draws itself from the dot to a rule, the name sitting on it. The most machine-voice — reads like the map annotating itself.
05 · THE NEON
The restaurant's own sign flickering on — bright glow-orange type with a two-blink entrance. The most night-city; earns its drama by appearing one at a time.

My read: 02 (Ghost) if you want the map to stay quiet, 05 (Neon) if you want hovering to feel like an event — and they pair: Ghost as the resting label on district-level names, Neon as the hover moment. 03 (Marquee) is the most "PlateMaps" on brand-voice grounds but the serif needs more pixels than a small hover tag gets. 04 is beautiful and probably too much drawing for something that appears and disappears constantly. 01 is the safe default if bubbles and tags must be visually one family.
