Map search bar

Treatments for the fly-to-a-spot field, drawn at rest on the night map, inside its white card. Nothing here is interactive and the dropdown is out of scope — this sheet only decides what the bar itself wears. Everything keeps the floor: 44px+ height, mono for machine text, muted #a7b0ba at 7.4:1 on the dark fill. Round two leads — no pill, no floating bubble, square corners, and the placement changes rather than just the paint.

Verdict

Where this sheet leans now

M, then O, then P. M is the real answer: once the field stops being an island and becomes the map's own top rail, the corner stops being contested and the small-screen second row disappears. O is M's cheap half if touching the Discover/Friends switch is off the table. P is the one that looks most like this app rather than like a generic dark UI — the mono label header is the restaurant page's voice. Q if the map should get quieter still, N if it should get quieter than that. R and T are bigger structural bets, and S only makes sense if near me is a feature you want on the map.

All eight drop the pill, so all eight are a deliberate break from the app's rounded chrome — the map is already DESIGN.md's confirmed exception, and square map furniture reads as instrument rather than as a mislaid cream-world control. Worth deciding that once, explicitly, before building any of them.

Round one's verdict, for the record. C was the pick there — it keeps the shipped geometry and simply stages the ember so the accent means something again; F is C plus a keyboard hint if the slot feels bare. B is the tasteful alternative if translucency over the tiles proves legible on real photos of the style — and H is B with the city's own two colors braided into the hairline if it should feel more expensive. D and E are the boundaries: one too loud, one borrowed from the wrong wardrobe. Of the second six, G, I, J and K are deliberate excursions off-system (each note says which rule it bends); L is orthogonal — a small-screen layout move that any winning skin can wear.
