About us

A neighborhood place

3N1 Sports Bar & Grill is a neighborhood place. We hope you will come in and enjoy our 20 beers on tap, or grab a cigar in our fully stocked humidor. We over 25 TVs to show every major sporting event. Our Lounge (21 and over) is a 1000 sq ft cigar area that is has a filtered ventilation system, lounge chairs, and tables to enjoy a game of backgammon or chess. We have a full bar with an intensive collection of Whiskeys and Tequilas. Our eating area offers bar-style tables and chairs. Our Menu offers many great foods and appetizers. We hope you will come to visit us soon.
