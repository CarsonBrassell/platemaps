Lighthouse: the PageSpeed Insights API was over its anonymous daily quota;
Speed Insights (added in this stage) will give field numbers instead once
deployed. Re-run this table after the region move to iad1.

Database side (Neon, listed rows = 9,023): the `restaurants` columns
`getRestaurants` selects total ~2.5 MB on disk, of which `hours` is 1.78 MB;
`hours` is read by the open-now filter, so it cannot be dropped from the
corpus without a redesign (S1 column trim is therefore small; the corpus
cache is now stale-while-revalidate instead).
