Our Restaurant

We are fresh Vietnamese breath in the beautiful Burnhamthorpe district. You can easily spot “I Love Pho” by our magnificent neon sign above our storefront. We welcome you to enjoy our delicious “I Love Pho” cuisine in a pleasant atmosphere every day from 10 am. We offer a wide variety of Pho made of fresh quality ingredients.
