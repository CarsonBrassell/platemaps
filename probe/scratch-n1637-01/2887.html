Gihon Ethiopian Kitchen

At GIHON Ethiopian Kitchen, we serve more than food — we serve tradition, culture, and community. What began in 1998 as a humble, family-owned restaurant in San Diego has grown into a vibrant takeout hub, mobile kitchen, meal prep, and catering service — all driven by one purpose: to share the authentic flavors of Ethiopia with everyone we meet. We use fresh, high-quality ingredients in every dish, staying true to our roots while delivering meals that nourish both body and soul.

FAQs

Do you offer delivery?
Yes! We deliver throughout San Diego. Whether it’s a one-time order or a weekly meal prep package, we’ve got you covered.
How do I place an order?
You can place an order online through our website. For custom catering or special requests, feel free to call or message us.
What’s included in the meal prep service?
Our meal prep includes authentic Ethiopian dishes like Doro Wot, lentil stews, veggies and injera. You can choose full or half packages based on your needs.
Do you offer vegan options?
Absolutely! We have a variety of delicious vegan-friendly Ethiopian dishes.
Can I customize my catering order?
Yes, we offer fully customizable catering packages for all occasions birthdays, holidays, office events and more.
