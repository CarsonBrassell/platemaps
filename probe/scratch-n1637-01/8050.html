Your Questions Answered

Find quick answers about our menu, services, and policies to make your visit smooth and enjoyable.

  • What are your opening hours?

    We’re open Monday to Friday from 8 AM to 2 PM, Saturday from 9 AM to 12 PM and we are closed on Sundays.

  • Is Wi-Fi available in the shop?

    Yes, free Wi-Fi is available for all customers.

  • Do you offer vegan or gluten-free options?

    We have several vegan and gluten-free choices clearly marked on our menu.

  • How can I place an order?

    Orders can be placed in-store, by phone, or through our online platform.

  • Do you have a loyalty program?

    Yes, earn points with every purchase and redeem rewards on future visits.
