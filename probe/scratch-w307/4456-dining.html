    • Make Reservations

    SAN DIEGO ZOO SANDWICH CO.

    • Location: Front Street
    • Menu

    Located just inside the Zoo entrance, San Diego Zoo Sandwich Co. offers hot breakfast, specialty sandwiches, soups from scratch, ice cream, and fresh salads.

    Open for breakfast 8:30 to 11 a.m.; open for lunch at 11 a.m.

    • Menu
