LandShark Bar & Grill – Gaslamp Quarter, San Diego

Fins Up for Fun, Food and Frosty Drinks

Fins up for food, drinks, and good times at LandShark Bar & Grill in the heart of San Diego’s Gaslamp Quarter. This lively spot blends classic American tavern fare with fresh seafood, tropical drinks, and an island-inspired vibe. Perfect for a casual lunch, festive dinner, or evening with friends, LandShark offers a dining experience that captures the city’s energetic downtown spirit.

Menu Highlights:

  • Signature LandShark burgers and fish sandwiches
  • Baja-style tacos and fresh salads
  • Just-caught seafood entrees
  • Craft cocktails, local microbrews, and ice-cold LandShark Lager

With something for every taste, LandShark Bar & Grill is a must-visit destination for locals and visitors exploring San Diego dining.

Thirsty? The bar menu includes a deep range of imported and domestic beers, local microbrews, craft cocktails, and - of course - plenty of ice-cold frozen drinks. Right here is also the best place to order a cold pint of our celebrated signature beer, LandShark Lager.

Weekly performances by talented local artists create a lively soundtrack for your meal. Whether you’re enjoying happy hour, dinner, or evening cocktails, the vibrant energy of the Gaslamp Quarter comes alive at LandShark Bar & Grill.

Phone: (619) 531-8744

Browse our menu, reserve a table, or ask about hosting a private event. LandShark Bar & Grill is ideal for casual meals, celebrations, and lively gatherings in downtown San Diego.

LandShark Bar & Grill FAQs

LandShark Bar & Grill FAQs

What is LandShark Bar & Grill?

A lively Gaslamp Quarter bar and grill serving classic American fare, seafood, and tropical drinks.

Does LandShark offer live music?

Yes, weekly live music enhances the dining experience.

What are the signature dishes?

LandShark is known for burgers, fish sandwiches, Baja tacos, salads, and freshly prepared seafood.

Is there happy hour?

Yes, with drink specials and small bites perfect for after work or pre-game gatherings.

Is LandShark inside Margaritaville Hotel San Diego?

Yes, conveniently located on-site for hotel guests and visitors.

Can I host events at LandShark?

Absolutely. Private parties and group events are welcome with customizable menus.
