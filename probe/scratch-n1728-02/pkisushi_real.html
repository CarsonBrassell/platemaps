Chef's Specials

Our restaurant's specials feature the finest seasonal ingredients, sourced from trusted local and global suppliers. Each dish is meticulously crafted, showcasing our chefs’ expertise and creativity. With a perfect balance of umami flavors, we curate a truly memorable dining experience.

Nigiri & Sashimi

With over forty years of tradition and experience, we pride ourselves on selecting the finest ingredients. Our expertly crafted nigiri and sashimi highlight precise cuts that let you savor each texture and flavor. Indulge in a memorable dining experience that honors true culinary artistry.

Sushi Rolls

At PKI Sushi, we take pride in crafting savory sushi rolls that delight your taste buds. Our experienced chefs prepare each roll with skill and passion, emphasizing balance and natural flavors, using light or no sauces to let the ingredients shine.

Kitchen Plates

Our restaurant seamlessly blends modern innovation with time-honored tradition—and no menu is complete without a robust selection from the kitchen. Our thoughtful range of dishes includes Chicken and Beef Teriyaki, comforting bowls of Tempura Udon, and savory Gyoza appetizers. Whether you're young or simply young at heart, there's something to delight every palate.

Spirits

Enjoy crisp beers like Sapporo, Kirin Ichiban, Orion, and Asahi Super Dry (bottle & tap). Savor hot or cold sake and the delicate sweetness of plum wine. Try premium Korean soju. Wine lovers can indulge in Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc, dry Riesling, Rosé, Cabernet, Pinot Noir, Red Blend, Malbec, or Sangiovese.

Special Occassions

We offer a semi-private indoor section with its own entrance—perfect for hosting celebrations, birthdays, graduations, and business gatherings for groups of 30 to 50 people. While our photos highlight the space itself, not the people in it, PKI has become a favorite spot for gatherings that are full of life, laughter, and connection. This comfortable room is designed for eating and interacting, while a charming patio walkway just outside offers guests a chance to step out, mingle, and get some fresh air. The shared outdoor path—reminiscent of a quiet side street in Japan—adds to the relaxed, welcoming atmosphere, set apart from parking lots and traffic.
We offer flexible buffet or table service options to suit your event’s needs and help make every gathering feel personal and memorable. A large screen inside the space can be used for photo slideshows or video montages, allowing you to celebrate special moments with your guests and share them—subtly—with the broader PKI Sushi community.

About Us

At PKI Sushi, we bring the heart of Japanese cuisine to RB in San Diego’s North County with fresh ingredients, time-honored recipes, inspired chef creativity, and warm hospitality. Our owner opened his first Japanese restaurant [YEARS] years ago, driven by a deep passion for authentic cuisine and a genuine desire to connect with people through food. Since then, his hands-on dedication to creating a welcoming, familiar atmosphere—where guests feel like regulars from the moment they walk in—has shaped everything we do.
We chose the name PKI—which means Practice, Kindness and Love—because it reflects the vibrant spirit we bring to every meal and the uplifting experience we hope to share with each guest. Today, that same spirit lives on in every dish we serve—crafted with care and a sense of connection to create a truly authentic Japanese-style dining experience.
