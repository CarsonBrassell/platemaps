# VoiceOver and assistive access

12 confirmed (0 critical, 0 major, 6 minor, 6 polish) · 0 deliberate · 0 refuted, of 12 reported. The finder rated the title finding major; I re-rated it minor. Nothing here is a likely rejection. All of it needs a real iPhone to judge the felt impact.

HIG pages read: voiceover and accessibility. Apple says: give each screen a unique, succinct title; use accurate headings; give custom controls a descriptive label and keep it current; notify VoiceOver when visible content changes; describe meaningful images. The accessibility page says to offer onscreen controls for gesture-driven actions and to avoid UI that dismisses itself on a timer. The voiceover page says nothing about focus or modals, so the modal items below rest on general principles and are kept modest.

## Findings

### AXS-01 · minor · Nine of the phone routes share the title "PlateMaps"
- **Where:** `src/app/m/layout.tsx:29-31` (title is the plain string "PlateMaps"). Only forgot-password, privacy, reset-password, terms and verify-email export their own metadata. Feed (`src/app/m/feed/page.tsx`), Discover (`src/app/m/page.tsx`), friends, account, settings, post, `restaurant/[id]` and `u/[id]` do not.
- **Screens:** Feed, Discover, Friends, Profile, Settings, Post a plate, Restaurant, Other profile
- **HIG:** voiceover: give each screen a unique title that says what it is. The title is what a screen reader reads first.
- **What happens:** I grepped `src/app/m` for `metadata` and `generateMetadata`. Six files match, and five of those are the routes above. Everything else inherits "PlateMaps". Next's route announcer reads `document.title` first, so each client navigation is announced as "PlateMaps". A restaurant page never says the restaurant's name.
- **Fix:** Add `export const metadata = { title: "Feed" }` (and the same for Discover, Friends, Profile, Settings, Post). Add `generateMetadata` to `restaurant/[id]` and `u/[id]`. Set `title.template` to `"%s · PlateMaps"` in `m/layout.tsx`.
- **App Review risk:** none
- **Needs device:** yes. Check whether the announcer is spoken inside the Capacitor WKWebView.

### AXS-02 · minor · Feed has no heading and no /m screen has a main landmark
- **Where:** `src/components/mobile/PhoneFeedHeader.tsx:55-58` (the header returns `null` when there is no count, and the "Food Feed" title was removed on purpose). `src/components/mobile/PhoneShell.tsx:50` wraps content in a bare `div`. No `<main` exists in `src/components/mobile`, `src/app/m`, `src/app/layout.tsx` or `src/app/m/layout.tsx` (grepped).
- **Screens:** Feed (the launch screen); the landmark gap applies to every /m screen
- **HIG:** voiceover: use accurate headings so people can understand the page structure; the rotor navigates by headings and other content types.
- **What happens:** The app opens on Feed. `PhoneFeedScreen.tsx` has no `h1` or `sr-only` heading, and `PhoneFeedTabs.tsx` has neither. VoiceOver has no heading to land on or jump to, and the rotor Landmarks list has no main.
- **Fix:** Render `<h1 className="sr-only">Feed</h1>` in `PhoneFeedScreen` (not in the header, which returns null). Change `pm-phone-content` in `PhoneShell.tsx:50` to `<main>`. This does not touch the visual design.
- **App Review risk:** none
- **Needs device:** yes. Check the rotor Headings and Landmarks lists on Feed.

### AXS-03 · minor · Rival toast: button named "Dismiss" hides the message, and the toast times out
- **Where:** `src/components/mobile/PhoneRivalToast.tsx:161` (`aria-label="Dismiss"`), timers at `:126` (PASSED_MS 6000, CHASE_MS 4500), the "See table" link at `:206-212`.
- **Screens:** Any screen after a post passes or nearly passes a friend
- **HIG:** voiceover: labels should describe the control and stay accurate. accessibility: avoid UI that auto-dismisses on a timer.
- **What happens:** The button holds "You passed Sam +2!", the points and "to pass X". Its `aria-label` replaces all of that with "Dismiss". The wrapper is `role="status"` but is mounted together with its content, so announcement is unreliable. The toast leaves after 4.5 to 6 seconds and holds the only inline "See table" link. The same information is on the Friends tab, so no task is lost.
- **Fix:** Drop the `aria-label` (visible text then names the button). Put the message in an always-mounted `sr-only` status region. Pause the timer while focus is inside the toast, or lengthen it for VoiceOver users.
- **App Review risk:** none
- **Needs device:** yes. Check what VoiceOver says when the toast appears.

### AXS-04 · minor · Filters and taste sheets never move focus in
- **Where:** `src/components/mobile/PhoneFilterSheet.tsx:536-573` (effect saves the opener, locks scroll, wraps Tab, restores focus on cleanup; no `.focus()` on mount; the comment at `:531-534` says focus is "moved in"). The same missing step in `src/components/mobile/PhoneTasteSection.tsx:71` (`role="dialog"`, no `focus()` call in the file).
- **Screens:** Discover filters sheet; taste sheet
- **HIG:** general principle (the voiceover page does not cover modals): when a modal opens, the screen reader and keyboard focus should move into it.
- **What happens:** The only `autoFocus` (`PhoneFilterSheet.tsx:318`) is on the address field, and only after "Use an address" is tapped. On open, VoiceOver focus stays on the button behind the scrim. `Dialog.tsx:147` shows the working pattern in the same codebase. The sheet has `aria-modal` and a labelled `h2`, which limits the damage on iOS 16 and up.
- **Fix:** After `const panel = panelRef.current;` add a focus on the first button or the heading (`tabIndex={-1}`). Do the same in the taste sheet.
- **App Review risk:** none
- **Needs device:** yes. Check whether `aria-modal` hides the page behind in this WKWebView.

### AXS-05 · minor · Map canvas has no name or pointer to the list alternative
- **Where:** `src/components/RestaurantMap.tsx:3034` (bare `div`, no role or label). The wrapper is `src/components/mobile/PhoneFeedMapPanel.tsx`.
- **Screens:** Feed, Map tab
- **HIG:** voiceover: describe graphics so their content is available. General principle: offer more than one way to do a core task.
- **What happens:** Pins are WebGL circles with no DOM. That is the recorded unclustered-pin map design, so I do not count it as a defect. The gap is that the container is silent, and nothing says that Discover is the accessible list of the same restaurants. Comment bubbles are DOM and are labelled.
- **Fix:** Give the container `role="region" aria-label="Restaurant map"`. Add one `sr-only` line before it: "Map of restaurants. To browse as a list, open Discover." with a link to `/m`.
- **App Review risk:** low (reviewers may test with VoiceOver on)
- **Needs device:** yes. Check what VoiceOver does when it lands on the map.

### AXS-06 · minor · Multi-photo carousel: no onscreen next/previous on phones, same alt on every slide
- **Where:** `src/components/feed/PostMediaCarousel.tsx:177-196` (arrows are `hidden ... sm:flex`), `:113-116` (alt builder), `:150-152` (group label).
- **Screens:** Feed cards with several photos, Restaurant page posts
- **HIG:** accessibility: offer onscreen controls for gesture-driven actions. voiceover: describe meaningful images.
- **What happens:** Below 640px the arrow buttons are `display:none`, so every iPhone has swipe only. VoiceOver's three-finger scroll and Switch Control scroll still work, and a "1/3" counter shows at `:199`, so this is not a blocked task. The alt string is `[dish, "at restaurant"].filter(Boolean).join(" ")`. A joined string is never nullish, so the `?? "Photo i"` fallback at `:116` is dead code, and every slide reads the same.
- **Fix:** Add the position to the alt: `${dish} at ${restaurant}, photo ${i+1} of ${n}`. Optionally show 44px arrows on phones.
- **App Review risk:** low
- **Needs device:** yes. Check swiping through the group with VoiceOver on.

### AXS-07 · polish · Vote, heart and save buttons flip their label and also set aria-pressed; the score is a labelled span
- **Where:** `src/components/feed/PostActions.tsx:295-304` (votes), `:171-172` (heart), `:210-211` (save), `:333-339` (score `span` with `aria-label`). Same pattern at `src/components/feed/CommentsScreen.tsx:520` and `src/components/PlateDetailSheet.tsx:592`.
- **Screens:** Feed cards, Restaurant posts, Comments
- **HIG:** voiceover: keep labels accurate and notify VoiceOver of content changes.
- **What happens:** "Remove upvote" with `aria-pressed=true` says the same thing twice. The state is carried by both. Using `aria-pressed` is a recorded design floor (DESIGN.md "Motion & accessibility"), so the flaw is only the label flip. The score `span` has no role, so whether WebKit speaks its `aria-label` is uncertain; it may read a bare number. Nothing speaks the new count after a vote.
- **Fix:** Fixed labels ("Upvote", "Downvote", "Heart", "Save") with `aria-pressed` for state. Add a `sr-only` suffix to the score instead of relying on `aria-label` on a span.
- **App Review risk:** none
- **Needs device:** yes. Check what VoiceOver reads for the arrows and the score.

### AXS-08 · polish · Card toasts are mounted with their text
- **Where:** `src/components/feed/PostActions.tsx:230-253` (`pointsToast`, `savedToast`, `shareNote` as `{cond && <span role="status">}`). Also `src/components/NavDot.tsx:18`.
- **Screens:** Feed cards, Restaurant posts
- **HIG:** voiceover: notify VoiceOver when visible content changes.
- **What happens:** The region and its text appear in the same render. Live regions are announced reliably only when text changes inside a region that already exists. `PostFlash.tsx:219` and `PhonePointsFly.tsx:118` do it right with always-mounted `sr-only` status paragraphs, and the points award is already covered by those, so the loss is mainly "Saved" and "Link copied".
- **Fix:** One always-mounted `sr-only` `role="status"` per card whose text is set on each action; mark the visual pills `aria-hidden`.
- **App Review risk:** none
- **Needs device:** yes. Check whether the Saved note is spoken.

### AXS-09 · polish · Form errors are not tied to their fields
- **Where:** `src/components/mobile/PhoneProfileAuth.tsx:282` (and `:262`, `:291` for the others; `aria-invalid` set, error `p role="alert"` at about `:331` has no id). No `aria-describedby` or `aria-invalid` at all in `src/app/m/post/page.tsx` or `src/components/account/RecoveryForms.tsx`.
- **Screens:** Sign in / Create account, composer, recovery forms
- **HIG:** general principle: label and describe fields so the problem is announced where the person is.
- **What happens:** Labels use `htmlFor`. The error is one form-level `role="alert"`, so it is spoken when it appears. Returning to a bad field then says "invalid data" with no reason.
- **Fix:** Give the message an `id` and add `aria-describedby` on the invalid field. Add `aria-required` to required inputs.
- **App Review risk:** none
- **Needs device:** no

### AXS-10 · polish · role="tab" controls have no tabpanel
- **Where:** `src/components/mobile/PhoneFeedTabs.tsx:78-86`, `src/components/feed/FeedSortSwitch.tsx:40`, `src/components/mobile/PhoneFeedMapPanel.tsx:471`.
- **Screens:** Feed tabs, sort switch, map data source switch
- **HIG:** general principle: a custom control should expose the role, state and keyboard behaviour of the control it imitates.
- **What happens:** `tablist` and `tab` with `aria-selected`, but no `tabpanel`, no `aria-controls`, no arrow-key roving. VoiceOver reads "tab, 1 of 3", which is acceptable. Hardware-keyboard users tab through each one.
- **Fix:** Add `role="tabpanel"` with `aria-labelledby`, or switch to `aria-pressed` buttons.
- **App Review risk:** none
- **Needs device:** no

### AXS-11 · polish · Map photo lightbox has no close button and does not restore focus
- **Where:** `src/components/RestaurantMap.tsx:3068-3083`; effect at `:3059-3066`.
- **Screens:** Map comment bubbles
- **HIG:** accessibility: offer an onscreen control for a dismiss gesture.

- **App Review risk:** none

### AXS-12 · polish · Pill nav variant has icon-only links
- **Where:** `src/components/mobile/PhoneNav.tsx:177-204` (label printed only when `current`, at `:202`). `NAV_VARIANTS` at `:38` includes "pill" and "strip", chosen by `?nav=`.
- **Screens:** Tab bar, only with `?nav=pill`
- **HIG:** voiceover: give key controls a descriptive label.
- **What happens:** Non-current pill links hold an `aria-hidden` icon and maybe a dot, so they have no name. The default "arc" bar is fine. A person in the app cannot reach the variant without a URL.
- **Fix:** Add `aria-label={slot.label}` to the `Link` in `PillSlot`, or delete the scaffolding as its comment plans. I did not read the strip variant.
- **App Review risk:** none
- **Needs device:** no
