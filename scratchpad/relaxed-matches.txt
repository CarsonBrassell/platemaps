Common thread: GENERIC_NAME_WORDS (RESTAURANT/CAFE/CAFFE/GRILL/PIZZA/DELI/
SHOP/TACO/BAR/KITCHEN/BAKERY/MARKET/INC/LLC) is only stripped when building
the "core" squashed string for the substring-containment check. It is not
applied when picking either side's "first long word" for the word-overlap
check, so a >=5-letter generic/category word (COFFEE, SWEET, PIZZA - none of
which are in that list) can carry a match on its own. All seven flagged pairs
above are squashed-name-fallback (1) or raw-token-fallback (710 GRILL) hits;
none of the fallback-2 near-number matches besides SEASIDE and 710 GRILL
looked questionable on inspection.
