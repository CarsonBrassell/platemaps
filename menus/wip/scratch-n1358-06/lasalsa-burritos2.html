Burritos

At La Salsa, burritos are more than just a meal — they’re a celebration of the bold, vibrant flavors of Mexico — re-imagined with our signature fresh-grill twist. We take time-honored recipes and elevate them using high-quality ingredients prepared fresh daily: from flame-grilled chicken, steak, or shrimp, to hand-chopped veggies, house-made salsas, and creamy guacamole. Whether you’re craving something classic and comforting or a freshly grilled specialty, our burrito lineup delivers big flavor, satisfying portions, and unmistakable La Salsa freshness in every bite.

Overstuffed Grilled Burrito

A heaping double-portion of chicken, habanero jack cheese, fresh salsa and guacamole all packed into a flour tortilla and grilled up to a two handed perfection.

NO Rice NO Beans™ Burrito

But lots of meat and veggies- grilled chicken, fajita veggies, zucchini, broccoli, carrots, corn and salsa combined with habanero jack cheese and poblano crema and then wrapped up tight in a flour tortilla.

Grande Burrito

A burrito that lives up to it’s name! Grilled chicken, rice, beans, jack and cheddar cheeses, fresh salsa, guacamole, lettuce and sour cream all stuffed into a flour tortilla.

Original Gourmet® Burrito

A true fresh-mex classic and one of our long time favorites. Grilled chicken, guacamole, jack and cheddar cheeses and fresh salsa, wrapped up and grilled in a flour tortilla.

California™ Burrito

One of the tastes that started it all… beans, avocado, La Salsa’s own rice, jack and cheddar cheeses, fresh lettuce and salsa, wrapped up in a flour tortilla.

Los Cabos Shrimp Burrito

This filling favorite features grilled shrimp, fire roasted fajita veggies, rice, cheese, cabbage, crema Mexicana, avocado and salsa filled in a flour tortilla.

Veracruz Burrito

Grilled shrimp or fish, salsa, habanero jack cheese, rice, Veracruz sauce, cabbage, avocado and poblano crema stuffed in a flour tortilla.

Green Chile Burrito

Fire roasted green chile, grilled marinated chicken breast, cheese, rice and zesty ranchero sauce stuffed in a flour tortilla.

Bean and Cheese

A traditional favorite with bean and cheese wrapped in a warm flour tortilla.
