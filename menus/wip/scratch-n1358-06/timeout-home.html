Southwestern College Dining & Event Services

More than just food and spaces, we provide the foundation for community at Southwestern College, serving everything from everyday bites to unforgettable events. We provide the essential services that fuel our campus community. Whether you’re grabbing a quick coffee between classes, arranging a professionally catered luncheon, or booking a premier venue for a large-scale celebration, you’ll find everything you need right here. We invite you to discover how our eateries, catering, and event spaces can support your daily needs and help create your next memorable occasion.

Event Venues

Your Next Event, Elevated — Discover Our Campus Spaces. From large-scale productions in our state-of-the-art Performing Arts Center to major community events at Devore Stadium, Southwestern College offers a diverse portfolio of premier venues. Whether you are planning a professional conference, theatrical performance, or an intimate outdoor gathering, our flexible spaces and modern amenities provide the perfect backdrop. Explore our venues and find the ideal setting to inspire, connect, and bring your vision to life.

Catering

From full-day conferences to morning team meetings, our catering services bring high-quality cuisine and inviting hospitality to your campus event. As the exclusive caterer for Southwestern College, we offer a diverse menu to match any taste and budget, served at any indoor or outdoor location on campus. When you partner with us, the revenue generated is invested directly back into the college, helping to support SWC students and programs. Let us help make your next event a delicious success—explore our menu and begin planning today!

The Eateries

Nourishing minds and appetites, our campus eateries offer a delectable array of dining options to satisfy every palate. Whether you need to fuel up with a Starbucks coffee and a quick snack from Tradewinds or you’re sitting down for a hot meal at the Time Out Café, we are committed to providing high-quality food and exceptional service. We invite our students, staff, and guests to explore our delicious offerings and find the perfect bite to eat on campus.
