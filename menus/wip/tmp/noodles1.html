About Noodles & Company Carlsbad in Carlsbad, CA

Noodles & Company Carlsbad at 2521 Palomar Airport Road in Carlsbad, CA is your welcoming fast casual restaurant destination for delicious lunch, dinner or snack time dining near you. Here, you'll enjoy our menu filled with delicious Uncommon Goodness sure to satisfy every craving, from Italian pasta classics (Rigatoni Rosa and Basil Pesto Cavatappi) and Asian stir-fry noodles (Japanese Pan Noodles, Spicy Korean Steak Noodles, and Pad Thai) to American favorites (like our world-famous Creamy Cheddar Mac and Cheese and our Chicken Noodle Soup), fresh salads, and wholesome kids meals. We also offer veggie-packed options, delicious salads, and tasty choices to fit any food lifestyle, from vegetarian to vegan, gluten-free to keto, and more. Each entrée is always freshly prepared, cooked to order, and made just the way you want.

Enjoy comfortable indoor seating or our relaxing patio seating and free WiFi. For convenience use our website or app for online ordering and order ahead for Quick Pickup or ask for "delivery near me".
